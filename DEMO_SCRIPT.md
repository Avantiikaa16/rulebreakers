# RuleBreakers — 3-minute demo script

Record at 1280×800+ in a real browser (not the E2E script). Turn sound on. Reset progress first
(`Reset progress` link on the island) for a clean run.

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook (voice-over, show the island then Dragon Bakery)
> "Every math app starts the same way: here's a question, find the answer. RuleBreakers does the
> opposite — no question. Just a small broken world."

## 0:15–0:35 — Notice
Enter **Dragon Bakery**. Let the alarm play (screen shakes, Baker Bramble screams). Show the three
dragons with uneven cookies. **Don't explain what's wrong yet — let the visual speak.**
> "Three dragons. Their cookies aren't shared fairly. No prompt tells the child that — they have to see it."

## 0:35–0:55 — Fix + incomplete explain
Tap cookies to even the dragons out. Click **I fixed it!**. Click **How did you know?** and type/say:
**"everyone gets the same amount"** — deliberately incomplete (doesn't mention using *all* the cookies).

## 0:55–1:25 — The AI's real job
Show the **"Rule so far"** card, then click **Next world**. A *new* Dragon Bakery appears with cookies
left on the counter.
> "The AI didn't give a random new problem. It read her rule, found the gap — she never said
> 'use all of them' — and built the smallest world that exposes exactly that."
Fix the leftover-cookie world, explain again: **"and you give out all of them, none left over."**
Show **RULE UPGRADED!**

## 1:25–1:50 — The trick
A third world appears that's already perfectly fair — no glitch. Click **Nothing's wrong 🤨**.
> "Some worlds aren't broken at all. If a kid always changes something, they've learned the wrong
> lesson. This one tests whether she can tell the difference."
Show **"You cracked the rule!"** with the full thinking-journey list.

## 1:50–2:15 — Teach it forward via the Boss (optional if time)
Cut to the island fully cracked → **Face The Glitch**: one scene, three glitches at once (fair
shares, fair pieces, steady steps). Fix all three quickly (speed up in edit if needed).
> "The Glitch Boss combines every rule at once — and sometimes plants a decoy that's already fine."

## 2:15–2:45 — Tutor Handoff Report
Open **/report**.
> "Every session becomes a reasoning profile — not a score. Which parts of the rule she found, in
> her own words, in order, and what to try next. In a Live + AI learning system, this is what a
> tutor reads before they even say hello."

## 2:45–3:00 — Close
> "RuleBreakers. We don't teach kids to answer questions. We teach them to notice when something
> doesn't make sense."
